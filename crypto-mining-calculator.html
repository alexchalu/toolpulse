📈 Mining Profitability Factors

Several factors affect your mining profitability:

  • Hashrate: Your mining device's computational power
  • Electricity Costs: The biggest expense in mining operations
  • Network Difficulty: How hard it is to mine blocks
  • Cryptocurrency Price: Directly impacts your revenue
  • Pool Fees: deducted from your earnings
  • Hardware Costs: Initial investment in mining equipment

This calculator provides estimates based on current market conditions. Actual results may vary based on network changes, electricity rates, and market volatility.
