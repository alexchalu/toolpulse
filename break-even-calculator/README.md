# Break-Even Calculator

A comprehensive online calculator for small business owners and entrepreneurs to determine their break-even point and analyze business profitability.

## Features

- **Break-Even Point Calculation**: Determine units and sales dollars needed to break even
- **Contribution Margin Analysis**: Calculate profit per unit and margin percentage
- **Time Period Analysis**: Monthly, quarterly, and annual break-even analysis
- **Profitability Status**: Real-time assessment of current business performance
- **Interactive Chart**: Visual representation of costs vs revenue
- **Business Insights**: AI-powered recommendations for improvement
- **Margin of Safety**: Calculate how much sales can drop before losses occur
- **Operating Leverage**: Understand financial risk and potential returns
- **Mobile Responsive**: Works perfectly on all devices
- **Real-time Calculations**: Instant updates as you input values
- **Professional Design**: Clean, modern interface with Bootstrap 5

## How to Use

1. **Enter Fixed Costs**: Rent, salaries, insurance, and other costs that don't change with sales
2. **Input Variable Cost per Unit**: Cost to produce each unit (materials, labor, etc.)
3. **Set Price per Unit**: Selling price of each unit
4. **Specify Target Profit**: Monthly profit goal (optional, defaults to 0)
5. **Add Current Sales**: Your current or expected monthly sales volume
6. **Choose Time Period**: Select monthly, quarterly, or annual analysis
7. **View Results**: Get instant calculations and insights

## Formulas Used

### Break-Even Units
```
Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price per Unit - Variable Cost per Unit)
```

### Break-Even Sales ($)
```
Break-Even Sales = Break-Even Units × Price per Unit
```

### Contribution Margin
```
Contribution Margin per Unit = Price per Unit - Variable Cost per Unit
Contribution Margin Ratio = Contribution Margin per Unit ÷ Price per Unit
```

### Margin of Safety
```
Margin of Safety = (Actual Sales - Break-Even Sales) ÷ Actual Sales × 100%
```
