What is ROI?

ROI (Return on Investment) measures the profitability of an investment. It shows the percentage return generated on your initial investment.

ROI = ((Gain - Cost) / Cost) × 100%

Investment Cost: The initial amount invested or spent

Investment Gain: The return or profit generated from the investment

Net Profit: The difference between gain and cost

ROI: The percentage return on your investment
