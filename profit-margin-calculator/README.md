# Profit Margin Calculator

A comprehensive online calculator for small business owners and entrepreneurs to calculate their business profitability metrics.

## Features

- **Gross Profit Margin**: Calculate profitability after direct costs
- **Operating Profit Margin**: Calculate profitability from core business operations  
- **Net Profit Margin**: Calculate overall profitability after all expenses and taxes
- **Real-time Calculations**: Instant updates as you input values
- **Break-even Analysis**: Determine your break-even point
- **Mobile Responsive**: Works perfectly on all devices
- **Professional Design**: Clean, modern interface with Bootstrap 5

## How to Use

1. Enter your total revenue in the "Total Revenue" field
2. Input your Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
3. Add your Operating Expenses
4. Include Interest Expense if applicable
5. Set your Tax Rate (defaults to 21%)
6. View real-time calculations of all profit margins

## Formulas Used

### Gross Profit Margin
```
Gross Margin = (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100%
```

### Operating Profit Margin
```
Operating Margin = (Gross Profit - Operating Expenses) ÷ Revenue × 100%
```

### Net Profit Margin
```
Net Margin = Net Income ÷ Revenue × 100%
```

### Break-even Point
```
Break-even Point = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin Ratio
```
