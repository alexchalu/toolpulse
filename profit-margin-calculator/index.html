Understanding Profit Margins

Gross Profit Margin

Meures profitability after direct costs of goods sold.

Gross Margin = (Revenue - COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100%
Operating Profit Margin

Meures profitability from core business operations.

Operating Margin = (Gross Profit - Operating Expenses) ÷ Revenue × 100%
Net Profit Margin

Shows overall profitability after all expenses and taxes.

Net Margin = Net Income ÷ Revenue × 100%
